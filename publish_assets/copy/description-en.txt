Turn your AI Passport into a Tesla key that fits in a pocket. Power it on and it finds your car, connects, and lets you lock, unlock, pop the frunk or the trunk, and even authorise driving — one button press each. It runs completely offline: no phone app, no network, so it still works deep in an underground garage. The home screen shows the live connection state and battery, and a long press opens a diagnostic page that tells you where things stopped. Set it up once by typing your VIN on your phone, then just walk up to the car. Verified on a real 2025 refreshed Model Y; other models have not been tested.
