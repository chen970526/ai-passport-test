1. First boot: the screen shows a setup QR code. On your phone, join the hotspot "AI-PASSPORT-TSL" (the password is printed on screen), scan the code or open http://192.168.4.2 in a browser, type your 17-character VIN and save. The device reboots after about 2 seconds.
2. Bind the car: first fully quit the Tesla app on your phone and stop it from relaunching in the background — the car only allows 3 devices to be connected at a time, and an app sitting in the background eats one of those slots, so the device either fails to connect or stops responding later. Then switch off your phone's Bluetooth, keep at most 3 keys on the vehicle, sit inside and wake the centre screen. The device scans and lists what it found — the cyan dot is most likely your car. Right-1 / Right-2 select it, Right-3 connects.
3. Once it connects, the car shows no prompt at all — that is normal. Hold the physical NFC key card on the reader in front of the centre cup holders, and only then does the car raise the authorisation prompt. Press Right-3 to confirm; once it says bound, the home screen loads.
4. Daily use: Right-1 locks, Right-2 unlocks and authorises driving, Right-3 single tap opens the frunk and double tap opens the trunk. Tap quickly — a long press never runs a vehicle action.
5. Hold Right-3 for the diagnostic page (connection state and logs). To reset or move to another car: press and hold Right-2 first, then add Right-1 and keep both held for 3 seconds — the screen says the data was cleared and the device reboots into first-time binding. Wiping the device does not wipe the car: also delete the old key on the touchscreen under Controls > Keys, because the car stores only a limited number of keys and full slots will make a new binding fail.
